PetPost

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

The short version

PetPost does not collect any of your information. Everything you create in PetPost stays on your own device. There are no PetPost servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your account (username, name, your pet's name and type), your password (stored only as an encrypted hash), your photos, videos, moments, licks and comments, your training goals, streaks and trophies, and your pet care needs are saved inside the app on your device. Nobody else can see them, including the people who make PetPost.

Camera, microphone and photos

PetPost asks to use your camera and microphone only when you take a photo or record a video to post, and your photo library only when you pick or save a photo or video. Nothing you capture leaves your device unless you share it yourself.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, PetPost schedules notifications on your device for your pet care needs. They are created on the device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Sharing and Daybloom

When you tap Share, Save or Send to Daybloom, PetPost hands the content to the app or website you choose. What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time: open your profile, tap Edit profile, then Delete account. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.

Children

PetPost does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/vibe331212/PetPost.
