PetPost

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

The short version

PetPost keeps your account and posts online so you can sign in on any device and friends can follow you. We only keep what the app needs to work. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your information.

What is stored online

Your username, name, password (handled by our sign-in service and never stored in plain text), your pet's name, type, breed, birthday and favorites, your bio and profile photo, the photos, videos, moments and captions you post, your licks, comments and follows, your training goals, trophies and streak days. These are stored with Supabase, our database and file host, in the United States.

Who can see it

Your profile, posts, licks, comments, follows, goals and trophies can be seen by other people signed in to PetPost. Your saved posts, care needs and settings are private: only you can see them.

Camera, microphone and photos

PetPost asks to use your camera and microphone only when you take a photo or record a video, and your photo library only when you pick or save one. Only the photos and videos you choose to post are uploaded.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, PetPost schedules your pet care reminders on your device. They are not sent anywhere.

Sharing and Daybloom

When you tap Share, Save or Send to Daybloom, PetPost hands the content to the app or website you choose. What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Safety

You can report posts and accounts, and block people so you no longer see each other's posts. Reports go to the people who run PetPost.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time: open your profile, tap Edit profile, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account, posts, photos, videos, comments, follows and goals.

Children

PetPost is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/vibe331212/PetPost.
